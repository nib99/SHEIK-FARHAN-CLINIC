Privacy notice

This website is operated for Sheik Farhan Traditional Medicine Clinic in Hirna Town, Oromia, Ethiopia. This initial notice describes the limited information collected through appointment requests.

Appointment requests

If you submit a request, the clinic receives your name, phone number, preferred date, optional reason for visit, selected language and consent record so staff can respond. Do not submit emergency details or sensitive medical information through the public form.

Purpose and access

Information is used to respond to your appointment request and coordinate clinic contact. Access should be limited to authorized clinic personnel. The operator must configure secure hosting, backups, retention and access controls before collecting real patient data.

Your choices

You may contact the clinic using 0915001743 or 0915030381 to ask about your request or personal information. Applicable legal rights and retention requirements may apply.

Medical information

This public website is not an emergency service and does not provide diagnosis or treatment advice. For urgent medical needs, contact the nearest appropriate health facility.

Deployment note: The clinic owner must review and approve this notice, identify the data controller and retention schedule, and obtain legal/privacy review before production patient-data collection.
