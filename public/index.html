FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Where is the clinic located?

We are in Hirna Town, on the road toward Hirna Hospital. From the hospital road, continue approximately 200 metres downhill from the hospital.

How do I book a visit?

Submit the appointment form or call either clinic number. Staff will contact you to confirm availability.

Can I ask about a product before visiting?

Yes. Contact the clinic to ask about product details, current availability and documentation.

Does the website provide medical advice?

No. Website information is general clinic information and is not a diagnosis or a substitute for qualified medical care.
